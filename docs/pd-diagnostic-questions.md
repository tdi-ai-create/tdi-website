# PD Diagnostic questions

Preserved from `/pd-diagnostic` before that page was retired in September 2026.
The page is gone, the questions are not. They are a strong discovery script for
a leader conversation, and the four options per question are deliberately laid
out as a progression from no system to a working system.

## 1. When PD ends, where does instructional support live the following week?

- **A.** Nowhere - teachers are on their own
- **B.** In the momentum from the session
- **C.** With instructional coaches (for some staff)
- **D.** Built into ongoing coaching, PLCs, and role-specific support

## 2. Is PD concentrated on specific days or distributed throughout the year?

- **A.** Concentrated on designated PD days
- **B.** Intensive whole-staff sessions with limited follow-up
- **C.** Ongoing for core teams, limited for others
- **D.** Ongoing and accessible for all staff year-round

## 3. Which staff groups receive the most consistent PD support?

- **A.** Core instructional staff only
- **B.** Everyone receives the same content
- **C.** Core staff get coaching; others get minimal support
- **D.** All staff receive role-specific, aligned support

## 4. Do specialists, paraprofessionals, and support staff receive role-specific learning?

- **A.** Rarely or inconsistently
- **B.** They attend the same sessions as teachers
- **C.** Sometimes, but not systematically
- **D.** Yes, with clear alignment to classroom expectations

## 5. Can leadership see evidence of PD application in classrooms?

- **A.** Limited or inconsistent evidence
- **B.** Strong evidence immediately after PD, then fades
- **C.** Clear evidence in coached classrooms only
- **D.** Consistent evidence across most classrooms

## 6. Is there a shared instructional and behavioral language across roles?

- **A.** Varies significantly by role and classroom
- **B.** Shared at a conceptual level, inconsistent in practice
- **C.** Strong among core staff, weak elsewhere
- **D.** Yes, used consistently building-wide

## 7. What happens when implementation stalls?

- **A.** Nothing systematic - it stays stalled
- **B.** We schedule another PD session
- **C.** Coaching helps some teachers, others struggle
- **D.** Support systems are already in place to address it

## 8. Would most staff describe PD as relevant to their daily work?

- **A.** Not really - it feels like a requirement
- **B.** Inspiring in the moment, hard to apply later
- **C.** Core staff say yes, others say no
- **D.** Yes, most staff find it directly applicable
