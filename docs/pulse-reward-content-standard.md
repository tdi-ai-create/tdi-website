# The Pulse — Reward Content Standard

**Scope:** every piece of content that can appear after a staff member taps their weekly check-in. Jokes, quotes, animal photos, gifs, puns, micro-facts, sound rewards, and any copy wrapped around them.

**Out of scope:** the check-in questions themselves (see the Vibe Check spec), Focus tools (see `docs/hub-content-standard.md`), and hub content.

**Status:** required. No item ships to a school without passing every section below.

---

## 1. The universal test

Every item must be safe to display, unannounced, on the phone of any staff member in any school TDI works with.

Three questions, all of which must be yes:

1. **Would this be fine on a bulletin board in the main hallway?** If it needs context, an inside joke, or a closed door, it fails.
2. **Does it work for someone who has never taught a lesson?** A para, a custodian, a bus driver, an office manager, a cafeteria worker, a nurse, a coach.
3. **Would a superintendent in the most conservative district we serve, and a superintendent in the most progressive one, both shrug at it?** If either would flinch, it fails.

If an item requires a judgment call, it fails. There is no borderline bucket. We have 75 items to fill and no shortage of safe ones.

---

## 2. Hard exclusions

None of the following appear in any item, in any form, including as background detail in a photo or gif.

**Religion**
- No deities, prayer, scripture, worship, or religious figures
- No religious holidays as the subject or the setup, including Christmas, Easter, Ramadan, Hanukkah, Diwali
- No "blessed," "thoughts and prayers," "angel," or similar as figures of speech
- Seasonal content is fine when it is seasonal and not religious: snow, autumn leaves, the first warm day

**Politics and current events**
- No parties, candidates, elections, legislation, or political figures
- No school-funding politics, union bargaining, testing mandates, or curriculum controversy
- No news events, disasters, or anything tied to a headline

**Discrimination and identity**
- No content that singles out or jokes about race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, body size, immigration status, or socioeconomic status
- No accents, dialects, or "types of people" as the punchline
- No stereotypes about any group, including groups inside the building: no jokes at the expense of paras, custodians, bus drivers, administrators, coaches, or any department
- No content about specific countries or cultures presented as unusual

**Language**
- No profanity, no substitutions ("frick," "what the heck no," asterisked spellings), no implied profanity
- No crude anatomy, bodily functions, or bathroom humor
- No innuendo, double meanings, or anything that reads two ways
- No slang whose meaning we have not verified is clean in current usage

**Adult subject matter**
- No alcohol, drinking culture, "wine o'clock," or "I need a drink after today"
- No drugs, vaping, smoking, or cannabis, including as a joke about coping
- No romance, dating, marriage jokes, or physical attraction
- No gambling, betting, or lottery framing

**Harm and distress**
- No violence, weapons, injury, or death, including cartoon violence
- No content referencing self-harm, suicide, eating, dieting, weight, or body image, even positively framed
- No medical or mental-health advice, diagnosis language, or clinical terms
- No lockdowns, safety drills, school violence, or anything adjacent

**Money and work conditions**
- No jokes about teacher pay, being underpaid, second jobs, or buying supplies out of pocket. True, widely felt, and the wrong thing coming from a vendor
- No burnout, quitting, counting days to retirement, or "is it Friday yet"
- No comparisons between roles or their value

**Rights and brands**
- No copyrighted characters, film or TV stills, song lyrics, sports team marks, or licensed imagery
- No brand names as the subject of a joke
- No memes whose origin we cannot trace to a licensed source

**Students**
- No content that makes students the punchline
- No specific student behavior, discipline, or "kids these days"
- Students may appear as a warm subject, never as a complaint

---

## 3. Tone

TDI is a vendor speaking into someone's hard day. Three failure modes to avoid.

**Punching down.** We do not joke about how tired, overwhelmed, or undervalued staff are. They are allowed to say it. We are not.

**Toxic positivity.** No "choose joy," no "attitude is everything," no "if you're tired, imagine how the kids feel." A staff member who just tapped *Rough* should not be told to cheer up.

**Trying too hard.** No exclamation stacking, no "YAAAS," no slang that will age out in six months. Warm and plain beats energetic.

Sentence case. No all-caps except for a single sound reward label. Under 25 words for text items.

---

## 4. Role neutrality

Every item passes unless it has been deliberately assigned to a role bucket.

**Banned assumptions in general-pool items:** lesson planning, grading, prep periods, "your classroom," "your students," curriculum, standards, parent-teacher conferences, report cards, teaching credentials.

**Safe universal ground:** Tuesdays, coffee, weather, hallways, the copier, the school year as a shape, animals, tiny facts, mornings, the smell of a school in August.

**Role buckets** are permitted for items written for a specific group and shown only to that group, once role targeting exists. Until then, the general pool is the only pool.

---

## 5. Images and gifs

- Animals, landscapes, food, weather, abstract motion, hand-drawn illustration
- No identifiable people, including children. No exceptions, and no stock photos of classrooms
- No text baked into an image unless we wrote it and it passes sections 2 and 3
- No flashing, strobing, or rapid cuts. Anything under three flashes per second, for photosensitivity
- Legible on a phone at arm's length, and still readable in dark mode
- Alt text on every image, written for a screen reader, not a keyword list

---

## 6. Jokes

- Knock-knock and one-line format only, so the payoff is fast
- Setup on the check-in screen, punchline after the tap
- The subject is the school day, not the people in it
- No joke requires knowing anyone's name, role, or district
- Read aloud before approval. If it needs a delivery to work, it does not work

---

## 7. Licensing

- Gifs are embedded from a licensed source (Giphy or Tenor API), never downloaded and re-hosted
- Photos come from Unsplash, Pexels, or a paid stock license, with the license recorded per item
- Jokes, quotes, puns and facts are written in-house. No sourcing from joke sites, no scraping
- Quotes attributed to a named person require a verified source. If we cannot verify the attribution, we do not use the quote
- Every item carries its source and license in the content record

---

## 8. Review

- Two reviewers per item, one of whom must not have written it
- Every item is checked against sections 2 through 7 as a checklist, not a vibe
- Anything that produces a "probably fine" is cut, not discussed
- Approved items are stamped with the reviewer initials and date in the content record

---

## 9. Removal and complaints

- Any staff member or leader can flag an item. Flagged items come out of rotation the same day, before review, not after
- A removed item is never returned without a written reason and a second two-reviewer pass
- Removals are logged with the flag reason, so patterns are visible
- One flagged item is a mistake. Three in the same category is a spec problem, and this document gets updated

---

## 10. Refresh

- The library is reviewed once a year before the August start
- Anything that has aged, dated itself, or drifted out of section 3 is retired
- Target inventory: enough items that no staff member sees a repeat within a school year
