## Two bugs this found, both fixed

**Spring's timeline had never rendered.** All nine events carried
`status: "done"`, and the renderer filters on `completed`, `in_progress` and
`upcoming`. Nine events fell through every filter, so the section printed three
empty columns reading "Nothing here" on the live dashboard. Addison is the only
partnership with semester records, so nothing else was affected. Now reads
"Done 9" with the real delivery record: Exec Session 1, Part 1, Part 2,
Observation Day 1, Observation Day 2, Virtual 3, Virtual 3b, Virtual 4, Exec
Session 2.

**Every date was a day early.** `new Date('2026-07-01')` parses as UTC midnight
and renders as 30 June in Chicago. Contract start read "Jun 30, 2026" and
Observation Day 1 read "Feb 23" when the note itself says the 24th. Dates a
school plans visits around. `formatRecordDate` now builds the date from its
parts in local time. Re-checked after the fix: "Jul 1, 2026", "Sep 30, 2026",
"Oct 7, 2026", "Dec 31, 2026", and "Observation Day 1 Feb 24, 2026".

## How I verified, and its limit

Read back through the DOM (`textContent` of the selected tabpanel) rather than
from screenshots.

The dashboard is gated behind `showDashboard`, which waits on an intro
animation, and that timer does not fire while the tab is backgrounded by
automation. The content renders correctly and is in the DOM; it just sits at
`visibility: hidden` until the animation the automated tab never runs. That is
why every screenshot this session showed the loading splash. It is an
automation artifact, not something a person hits, and Rae confirmed the page
loads normally in her own browser earlier today.

So: the content and the structure are verified. The visual rendering of these
two panels is not, and is worth one look on production.
