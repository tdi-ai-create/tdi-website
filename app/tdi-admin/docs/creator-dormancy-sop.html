Creator Dormancy & Offboarding

Standard Operating Procedure -- What happens when a creator goes quiet, and what to do if they never come back

Last updated: August 13, 2026 | Written after the re-engagement audit

Overview

Creators sign up with real intent and then life happens. Most of the Creator Studio roster is dormant at any given time, and that is normal rather than a failure. This SOP covers what the system does on its own, what Bella does, and how a relationship ends when it needs to.

The guiding principle: a creator should never be surprised, and should always have a way back that takes one click.

Why this document exists. An audit on August 13, 2026 found the re-engagement sequence had been emailing the wrong people for a month, and nobody could tell, because there was no way to see the pipeline from the admin. The automation is now correct and visible. This SOP records the policy behind it, which until now lived only in people's heads.

What counts as dormant

Dormancy means no portal activity. Specifically the field last_portal_activity_at, which is stamped in exactly one place: when a creator opens their Creator Studio dashboard.

SignalCounts as activity?Why
Opening the portal Yes The creator did something. This is the signal the sequence starts and stops on.
Submitting a milestone Yes They had to open the portal to do it, which stamps the field.
An admin editing their record No Bella fixing a course title is not the creator coming back.
Replying to one of Bella's emails No, not automatically Inbound replies are not captured anywhere yet. Use Mark as Engaged by hand.

Never use updated_at to judge whether a creator is active. A database trigger stamps that column on any write to the record, including bulk scripts. A bulk edit on July 19, 2026 enrolled nineteen creators into a six week email sequence in a single day, none of whom had actually changed behaviour that week. If you are writing a query or a report about creator activity, use last_portal_activity_at.

The ladder

After 15 days with no portal activity, the system starts a sequence. One step per week, seven steps, all from Bella. Any portal activity at any point cancels the rest of it immediately.

Day 15 Step 0 "Just checking in." Warm, no ask, no pressure.
Day 22 Step 1 "Even 15 minutes can shift your momentum."
Day 29 Step 2 "Everyone hits a pause." Invites a reply about what is in the way.
Day 36 Step 3 "Your idea is still a great one." Offers to move the timeline.
Day 43 Step 4 "The door is open whenever you're ready."
Day 50 Step 5 Last weekly check-in. Warns that account next steps follow.
Day 57 Step 6 Pause notice, and the account is paused automatically. Carries a one-click reactivation link that works without signing in.

Once a sequence completes or is cancelled, that creator cannot be re-enrolled for 90 days. This stops someone who paused and came back from immediately restarting the ladder.

Pausing is not an ending. The pause email says so explicitly, and it is true: work and progress are saved, the affiliate link keeps earning, and the reactivation link brings the account straight back. Roughly one in three creators who have paused did it themselves, on purpose, which is the system working as intended.

What Bella does

Everything above runs without anyone touching it. Bella's job is to catch the people the machine should not be emailing, and to be a human where a human matters.

1 Check the Re-engagement tab weekly

Admin Portal > Creator Studio > Re-engagement

The tab shows the whole ladder at once: who sits on each step, who the next run would start a sequence for, who is due their next email, who is currently paused and for how long, and every re-engagement email that has gone out.

The one number worth watching is Facing pause. Anyone in that count is one week away from having their account paused automatically.

2 Read the "why" before acting

On the Creators tab, every stalled row explains itself underneath the name, for example "last milestone Feb 18, never signed in". Filter by Re-engagement to see just the people in a sequence.

If the reason looks wrong, that is a signal worth raising rather than ignoring. It is exactly what nobody could see before August 2026.

3 Use Mark as Engaged when you have made real contact

Mark as Engaged on the creator's detail page cancels the sequence and records the touch.

Use it when a creator replies to an email, answers a call, or tells you in person that they are coming back. The system cannot see any of that on its own, so without this they keep receiving nudges after they have already talked to you, which reads as nobody listening.

4 Reach out personally to anyone who got close

A creator at 30 of 40 milestones who went quiet is a different situation from one who never completed anything. The ladder treats them identically. You should not.

Before someone with real progress hits step 6, send them something personal. The automated emails are warm but they are not from a person who knows what they were working on.

Creators who are paused

Paused creators hear from Bella every 90 days, automatically, with a one-click reactivation link. That check-in exists so that pausing is a rest rather than a disappearance.

Paused creators are the easiest people in the whole system to forget. Between May and August 2026, six paused creators received nothing at all, because the check-in job stamped its own "already checked in" marker without ever sending an email. Two of them were at 32 and 38 of 40 milestones. If the paused list in the Re-engagement tab shows people at 90+ days with no recent check-in, something is wrong with the job and it should be raised, not waited out.

Ending a creator relationship

There are four ways to end a creator relationship. Only one of them tells the creator anything.

Pause Creator Bella System

Use this by default. Reversible, the creator is told, their work is saved, the affiliate link keeps earning, and they get a link that brings them straight back. Can be done by the creator themselves from their dashboard, by an admin, or automatically at step 6.

Withdrawn Bella Rae

Use when a creator has actively told you they are not continuing. It records that decision and removes them from the public creators page.

No email is sent. If someone withdraws, reply to them personally. They told you something; the system will not answer on your behalf.

Archived Bella Rae

Housekeeping only. Hides a finished or abandoned record from the working list and removes them from the public creators page. Use for records you want out of the way but not gone.

Delete Rae

Permanent and unrecoverable. Destroys the creator and every milestone, note, project and submission attached to them. No email, no export, no undo.

Reserve this for duplicates and test accounts. It should never be how a real person's relationship with TDI ends.

Never delete a real creator to tidy up the roster. Use Archived. A dormant creator who reappears in eighteen months with their work intact is a good outcome; one whose entire history was destroyed is not.

Decided and still open

Recorded honestly so the next person knows which parts are settled.

Decided (Rae, August 13, 2026)

  • Activity means portal activity, not record edits
  • Auto-pause at step 6 stays automatic, now that the reactivation link works
  • Dormant unsigned creators are handled by the sequence rather than a manual triage pass
  • Paused creators get a real check-in every 90 days

Still open

Whether an unsigned creator is a creator. As of August 2026, twenty of twenty six active creators had never signed an agreement, and most had never signed into the portal. Deciding whether those records belong on the roster at all would shrink the dormancy problem more than any automation. Until that is decided, the ladder treats them the same as everyone else.

Whether a human should always make contact before an account is auto-paused. Currently a creator can go from signup to paused without any person speaking to them. Step 4 above is the manual habit that covers this, but it is a habit rather than a rule the system enforces.

Whether Withdrawn and Archived should collapse into one "Closed" state, and what a closed creator is told about their work.
