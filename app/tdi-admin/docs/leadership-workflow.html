Leadership Dashboard Workflow

White-glove partnership management from signing through renewal

Last updated: July 30, 2026

The Partnership Lifecycle

Quote Signed→ Partnership Created→ Principal Invited→ Roster Uploaded→ Hub Provisioned→ Ongoing Support→ Renewal

Phase 1: After a Quote is Signed

What happens automatically:

  • Partnership record created (if new school)
  • Contract deliverables generated from the quote line items
  • Receipt email sent to the signer with PDF agreement attached
  • Slack notification to sales and billing channels

What you need to do:

  • Verify the partnership shows up in Lead Dashboard
  • Set the contract phase (IGNITE, GROW, SUSTAIN, TRANSFORM)
  • Set session counts (observations, virtual, executive) based on the package
  • Add the contract start and end dates

Phase 2: Onboarding the Principal

  1. Go to Lead Dashboard > [School Name]
  2. Click the Invite button (top right area)
  3. Enter the principal's name and email
  4. The system creates their portal account and sends a welcome email
  5. The principal gets a link to their Partner Dashboard where they can upload their roster

Check invite status: The dashboard shows whether the principal has accepted their invite and logged in. If they haven't within a few days, follow up personally.

Phase 3: Staff Roster & Hub Access

  1. The principal uploads their staff roster through their Partner Dashboard
  2. The system automatically provisions Hub accounts for each staff member
  3. Welcome emails are sent to each staff member
  4. Staff appear in the Staff Roster section of the Leadership Dashboard

Over-contract alert: If the roster has more staff than the contracted seat count, you'll get a notification. This could be a replacement (no action needed) or an expansion opportunity.

Staff Photos

  • Individual: Click a staff member's photo area and upload
  • Bulk: Use Bulk Upload Photos with a ZIP file of photos named by email or name
  • Photos are auto-resized to thumbnail (64px) and full (400px)
  • Consent checkbox required for bulk imports

Recording a Session

  1. Go to the Billing tab
  2. Click Record Session on the appropriate service type
  3. Fill in the date, notes, and any teacher quotes
  4. Click Complete
  5. The system updates the used count, creates a timeline event, and notifies the team via Slack

Observations

  1. Go to the Observations tab
  2. Create a new observation visit entry
  3. During the visit, take notes (can upload photos of your notepad)
  4. After the visit, add strategy tags, growth indicators, and coaching themes
  5. Draft Love Notes (positive teacher observations to share with the principal)
  6. Review and finalize Love Notes before sending

Prepare for Call

Click "Prepare for Call" button to generate a one-pager with talking points based on:

  • Recent action items and their status
  • Staff engagement data from the Hub
  • KPI progress
  • Upcoming deliverables

Phase 5: Invoicing & Billing

See the Service Invoicing team doc for the full invoicing workflow. Key points:

  • Deliverables are auto-created when a quote is signed
  • Mark deliverables as "delivered" when sessions are completed
  • Generate and send invoices from the Billing tab
  • Follow-up emails go out automatically at 14, 30, 45, and 60 days
  • Record payments when received (method, check number, amount). The principal automatically gets a "Payment Received" confirmation email.

Phase 6: Renewal

Start renewal conversations 60-90 days before contract end.

  • Review KPI progress and impact data
  • Generate a Board Summary report for the principal to share with their board
  • Highlight engagement metrics, sessions completed, and teacher growth
  • Create a new quote with renewal terms
  • The system tracks contract expiration dates and sends alerts

White-Glove Standards

Every touchpoint matters. These are non-negotiable:

  • Respond to partner emails within 24 hours (business days)
  • Log every interaction in Internal Notes or Meetings
  • Update action items after every call or email
  • Always prep with "Prepare for Call" before scheduled meetings
  • Send Love Notes after every observation visit
  • Follow up on overdue action items within 48 hours
  • Never let a principal feel forgotten. If nothing is happening, check in anyway.
