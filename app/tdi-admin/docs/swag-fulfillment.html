Swag Shop Order Fulfillment

Owner: Bella Dailey | Last updated: July 2026 | Location: teachersdeserveit.com/swag

Overview

When a customer places an order on the TDI Swag Shop, Stripe processes the payment and an email notification is sent to the team. Bella is responsible for creating the order in Printful so it gets printed and shipped to the customer.

Key concept: We do NOT hold inventory. Printful prints each item on demand and ships directly to the customer. Our job is to transfer the order details from the notification email into Printful.

The Full Workflow

What happens automatically:

  1. Customer browses the swag shop at /swag
  2. Discount code TEAMTDI (20% off) is auto-applied
  3. Customer adds items to cart and clicks Checkout
  4. Stripe processes the payment at the discounted price
  5. Customer sees "Order confirmed!" success page
  6. Notification email is sent to hello@teachersdeserveit.com (cc: rae@teachersdeserveit.com)

What Bella does (manual steps):

  1. Check your email. You'll receive an email with subject "New Swag Order #XXXXXX" from TDI Swag Shop. This email has everything you need.
  2. Log into the Printful DASHBOARD. Go to printful.com/dashboard (NOT tdi.printful.me, which is the customer storefront with higher prices).
  3. Create a new order. Click "New Order" or go to Orders > Create Order. You are creating an internal order at wholesale/base cost, not buying from the retail storefront.
  4. Add each product. Search for the product by name. Select the correct:
    • Product (match the name exactly)
    • Color (if specified in the email)
    • Size (S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL as listed)
    • Quantity
  5. Enter shipping address. Copy the shipping address from the email exactly as shown.
  6. Review the order. Printful will show you their base cost. This will be LOWER than what the customer paid. That difference is our margin. This is normal.
  7. Submit the order. Printful will charge the TDI account for the base cost, then print, pack, and ship.
  8. Customer gets tracking. Printful automatically sends a shipping confirmation email with tracking info to the customer. You don't need to do anything for this step.

What NOT to Do

Do NOT fulfill an order if the Stripe payment shows as refunded or disputed. Check Stripe first.

  • Do NOT create discount codes in Printful or Stripe without Rae's approval
  • Do NOT change product prices on the website
  • Do NOT respond to customer shipping questions directly. Printful handles all shipping communications.
  • Do NOT fulfill orders for products we don't carry. If a product name in the email doesn't match anything in Printful, flag it to Rae.

Common Situations

Customer wants a refund

Escalate to Rae. Refunds are processed through Stripe, and the Printful order may need to be cancelled if it hasn't shipped yet.

Customer says they didn't get tracking

Check the order in Printful. If it shows "shipped," the tracking email may have gone to spam. You can resend the tracking info from Printful.

Wrong size/item ordered

If it hasn't shipped in Printful, cancel and recreate with the correct details. If it already shipped, escalate to Rae for a replacement order decision.

Bulk order inquiry

These come through /contact and are handled by Rae directly. Do not process bulk orders through the regular swag flow.
