Your Goals

Written with you on your onboarding call
Staff using the Hub in their own classrooms 24% of 70%

Checkpoint at the October call is 30 percent.

How we measure this

The share of your staff who have signed in and actually used the Hub, not just been handed an account. It comes from your Hub activity records, so nobody has to fill anything in for us. Today that is 24 percent, four of your seventeen staff, and two of those four started on the day of our call.

The harder bar sits just above this one. Nobody at Roosevelt has yet marked a resource as tried or adapted in their own classroom, which is the signal that something actually reached students rather than being read. That is what the goals below depend on, and it is the reason the one tool in three days ask matters.

Checkpoint at the October call is 30 percent, and the goal by the March renewal is 70 percent. These statements are drafts from our call. Tell us what to change and we will.

What this does not show

This goal is measured by asking your team rather than by watching a classroom, because nobody from our side is in your building this year. That is an honest limit of the plan you have, not a problem with the goal.

MTSS strategies in continuous use Target 60%

No number yet. This one starts moving once the Hub is in use.

How we measure this

The share of your staff who have tried or adapted an MTSS aligned strategy in at least two separate months, rather than once. It comes from the responses your staff mark against each Quick Win in the Hub, tagged to progress monitoring, assessment and intervention, counted per person per month. Nothing extra to fill in.

The two month rule is the whole point. One person marking five strategies in a single week after a staff meeting is not continuous practice, and we would rather not count it as though it were. You named progress monitoring checklists first on the call and MTSS as the initiative they sit inside, so this measures whether the strategies keep being used, not whether anyone says they own a routine.

There is no number yet because the Hub is barely in use. Goal by the March renewal is 60 percent.

Staff supported on stress and burnout Target 3.5 of 5

No baseline yet. Needs enough staff active for an average to mean anything.

How we measure this

Your school level Vibe Check average, out of 5. The Hub asks staff short check ins on mood, energy, belonging, purpose and needs, so this comes from what your team is already telling us rather than from another survey landing in their inbox. You named stress and burnout second on the call and it is already written into this partnership goal.

Individual vibe check and quiz results are private to the educator. You see the school level picture and never a named result. That is deliberate. If staff believe their principal can read their score they will not answer honestly, and the number stops being worth having.

There is no baseline yet because too few staff have signed in for an average to mean anything, so this one moves only once the first goal does. Goal by the March renewal is 3.5 out of 5.

Positive parent engagement Target set in October

Deliberately unset. We agree this one with you at the October call.

Why there is no number yet

Whether families hear from school when something is going well, not only when there is a problem. There is no number on this one yet, on purpose. You said on our call that you want this to support the work Roosevelt already runs rather than land as a separate initiative, and parent engagement is one of the priorities you named.

We would rather agree a target with you at the October call, once you have told us where families actually are, than put a figure on your dashboard that neither of us can stand behind today. Where the number comes from is part of that same conversation. Most likely a count of the positive family contacts your staff log, set alongside whatever you already track on family response.

These are drafts from your onboarding call. Tell us what to change and we will.
