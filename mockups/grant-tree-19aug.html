Changed today

What is new, and running

All four are live on production and were confirmed by reading the database, not the response.

  • The stop rule runs on a schedule It existed since 17 Aug but only fired when a draft was requested. Two of twenty-six paths had ever been screened. Now eighteen are, and it re-runs on the first of each month. Live
  • "Needs an answer" reaches a person The rule could conclude that someone must confirm something and then tell nobody. Six real questions now exist, one per grant path, across three schools. Live
  • "Stop this path" stops it It was a valid answer that nothing acted on. A path could be ruled out and stay open for an agent to keep drafting. Answering now closes it, keeping the answer, who gave it and when. Closed, never deleted. Live
  • Five portal pages became three Portfolio and Queue were rearrangements of what the main page already showed. Queue is now a filter. The word "pursuit" is gone from every screen; it was always a school. Live

What still needs adjusting

Ordered by what it costs.

  • Funder discovery ran for the first time It had never produced a single row. The insert wrote to a column called notes, and that table has no notes column, so every attempt failed with the error going to a console nobody reads. One word. Three briefs now exist and are waiting on an agent. Live 19 Aug
  • The eight QA checks do not exist Referenced once as a number and never enumerated. There is no rubric behind it. Open
  • Account setup is not a real step Two schools are blocked on a Deed account rather than on writing. It exists only as a follow-up titled "check if they set it up". Open
  • The money view stops at awarded Allocated and invoiced are not shown, and no path has awarded money yet. Open
  • Proficiency figures still have no source Reading and maths numbers are typed by hand, and every narrative and eligibility judgement rests on them. One school's cannot be verified at all. Open
  • The stop rule reasons from facts, never from scores Deliberate. Two of three live schools carry an unverified achievement figure, so a rule leaning on one would be worse than no rule. By design
  • A stopped path is answered, not deleted Deliberate. It keeps its reason, its decider and its date, and one click reopens it if the facts change. By design

Worth recording

One thing that went wrong today

The first live run of the audit reported questionsRaised: 6 and wrote none. Two CHECK constraints rejected every insert, and the count had been taken when the code decided to raise a question rather than when the database accepted one.

It was caught by querying for the rows instead of trusting the response. Both are fixed, and the run now reports what was written and surfaces what failed. The same bug class appeared four separate times today across payments, the stress chart, contact saving, and this. A write fails, the failure is swallowed, the caller reports success.
