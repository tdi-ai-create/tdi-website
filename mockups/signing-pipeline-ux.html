4. Enhanced Post-Signing Confirmation (What the Client Sees)

What exists today: After signing, Amanda sees a static card with "Agreement Approved", her name, the date, the selected package amount, and "Our team will be in touch shortly to coordinate next steps."

What already exists later in the flow: The partner-setup page (triggered by invite link) has a 3-step onboarding wizard (account creation, org info, roster upload) that ends with "What happens next" showing: (1) educators get Hub access within 24 hours, (2) dashboard updates in real time, (3) TDI will schedule a check-in call. The welcome email has 3 steps: log in, add your team, watch your team grow.

Recommendation: Bridge the gap between signing and the partner-setup invite. Right now the confirmation page is a dead end. Add a preview of what's coming so Amanda isn't just waiting for an email.
