Three ways a TDI voice can appear on a tool page

Same posting rules in all three. A TDI voice asks, prompts, and answers, and every second post ends in a question. It never claims a classroom moment that did not happen. What changes below is only how obvious it is that the account is ours. Sample posts, not real ones. Fonts are a stand in for DM Sans.
